01 / Who logs in

## Five people. Five screens. Nobody sees extra work.

__

**In simple words** The admin sees money. The teacher sees classes. The parent sees their child. Same institute — different doors.

  1. 1

**You add a person** Pick their job: admin, helper (sub-admin), teacher, student or parent.

  2. 2

**They get their own login** WhatsApp invite or Google sign-in. They never share one password.

  3. 3

**Their screen only shows their job** A helper / sub-admin cannot open fees unless you tick that box.

02 / Institute & people

## Build the school once. Then just add students.

__

**In simple words** Add classes, batches and subjects as three separate lists. They join when you enrol a student or add a teacher: pick the batches, then pick subjects for each batch.

  1. 1

**Set up your campus** Then add class names, batch names and subject names on their own. One campus per institute.

  2. 2

**Attach a fee plan** Pick the plan when you enrol the student. Discounts sit on the student.

  3. 3

**Enrol people** For each student or teacher: select batches, then subjects against each batch. A WhatsApp link can open their app.

05 / Live classes

## Students tap Join. Zoom opens. That’s it.

__

**In simple words** You connect your Zoom once. The LMS makes a private room for each class. Students never hunt for a meeting link.

  1. 1

**Set the timetable** Once a week, or “every Tuesday 4pm”. Rooms are created for you.

  2. 2

**Teacher starts the class** Countdown, then Start. Meet or Teams work the same way.

  3. 3

**Student taps Join** The button appears at the right time in their local clock.
