Why IGNITE LMS

Everything your institute needs, in one place

Eliminate the patchwork of disconnected tools. IGNITE covers every workflow, from first enrolment through graduation.

Course management

Build classes, batches and schedules with student enrolment, teacher assignment and progress tracking in one place.

Most used

Zoom-Integrated Live Lectures

Schedule, launch and monitor Zoom sessions from inside the platform. Attendance syncs automatically from participant reports.

Automated Fee Challans

Generate monthly challans in bulk, track payment status in real time, and send instant reminders to students and guardians.

Comprehensive Salary Management

Fixed, per-lecture, hourly or percentage-based pay. Payroll computes automatically and PDF receipts generate on demand.

Assignments

Teachers publish work, students submit digitally, and grades return automatically -- closing the feedback loop.

Quizzes & Exams

Question banks, timed papers and auto-grade -- quizzes for practice, exams when it counts, scores on the parent portal.

Real-Time Notifications

In-app alerts reach every stakeholder instantly -- lectures, assignments, fee challans and admin updates the moment they're issued.

Recurring Schedule Builder

Configure daily, weekly, monthly or fully custom lecture series. All sessions and Zoom meetings generate automatically in one step.

Analytics

Real-time dashboards on attendance, fee collection, performance and payroll -- for every role that needs them.

Global

Worldwide Time-Zone Lectures

Live lectures display in each participant's local time zone, so students across Pakistan, Bangladesh, India and beyond always join at exactly the right moment.
