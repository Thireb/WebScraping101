01 / Who logs in

Five people. Five screens. Nobody sees extra work.

In simple wordsThe admin sees money. The teacher sees classes. The parent sees their child. Same institute — different doors.
  1. 1
    You add a personPick their job: admin, helper (sub-admin), teacher, student or parent.
  2. 2
    They get their own loginWhatsApp invite or Google sign-in. They never share one password.
  3. 3
    Their screen only shows their jobA helper / sub-admin cannot open fees unless you tick that box.

02 / Institute & people

Build the school once. Then just add students.

In simple wordsAdd classes, batches and subjects as three separate lists. They join when you enrol a student or add a teacher: pick the batches, then pick subjects for each batch.
  1. 1
    Set up your campusThen add class names, batch names and subject names on their own. One campus per institute.
  2. 2
    Attach a fee planPick the plan when you enrol the student. Discounts sit on the student.
  3. 3
    Enrol peopleFor each student or teacher: select batches, then subjects against each batch. A WhatsApp link can open their app.
